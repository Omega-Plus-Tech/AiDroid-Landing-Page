Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

Thank you for using DressMe: AI Try On & Wardrobe, operated by AIDROID LLP. This Refund Policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for subscriptions and in-app purchases made through our App.

1. Purchases Through App Stores

All subscriptions and in-app purchases for DressMe are processed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Because payments are handled entirely by these platforms, refunds are subject to their respective policies:

  • Google Play: You may request a refund through Google Play's refund process within the eligible timeframe. Visit your Google Play order history or contact Google Play support.
  • Apple App Store: You may request a refund through Apple's refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple reviews each refund request individually.

AIDROID LLP does not directly process or approve refunds for purchases made through app stores. Please contact the respective app store support for assistance.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your app store account settings:

  • Android: Open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DressMe → Cancel.
  • iOS: Open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → DressMe → Cancel Subscription.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You will continue to have access to premium features until the subscription period expires. No partial refunds are issued for unused time within a billing cycle.

3. Free Trials

If a free trial is offered, it converts automatically to a paid subscription at the end of the trial period unless cancelled before the trial expires. We recommend cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

4. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally non-refundable:

  • Consumed in-app credits or one-time purchases that have already been used.
  • Subscriptions where the billing period has already started and premium features have been accessed.
  • Purchases made more than the app store's eligible refund window.

5. Exceptional Circumstances

If you believe you were charged in error or experienced a technical issue that prevented you from using the Service, please contact us at support@aidroid.co.in with your details. We will review your case and, where appropriate, work with the app store on your behalf or offer an alternative resolution such as extended access or credits.

6. Pricing Changes

We reserve the right to change subscription prices at any time. Price changes will be communicated in advance through the App or your app store. Continued use of the subscription after a price change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the new price.
