Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

Welcome to Perfect Pour: Brain Game ("App", "Game", "Service", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application and related services.

In short: we do not collect personal information. The App does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you personally.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, request, or store:

  • Your name or any player identity details
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Account credentials (the App has no accounts or sign-in)
  • Photos, contacts, location, or files from your device
  • Payment card details

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Your gameplay information is saved locally on your own device and is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Level progress and completed puzzles
  • Scores, stars, streaks and achievements
  • In-game coins, hints and power-ups
  • Game settings and preferences (such as sound and music)

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting the App removes it. If your device backs up app data through Google or Apple, that backup is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

The App uses third-party services for advertising, crash reporting, and analytics. These services may automatically collect limited technical information, which is not used by us to identify you personally:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifier
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Anonymous usage signals, such as levels played and session length

4. How This Information Is Used

The limited information described above is used to:

  • Operate and maintain the game experience.
  • Diagnose crashes and fix technical issues.
  • Balance puzzle difficulty and improve level design.
  • Display advertisements and measure their performance.
  • Process in-app purchases through the app stores.
  • Detect fraud, cheating and ensure platform security.

5. Advertising

The App may display advertisements, including rewarded video ads that grant in-game rewards. Advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads.

You can limit or reset personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings — on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases, if offered, are processed entirely through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another authorized payment provider.

We do not collect, see, or store your payment card information. Payment details are handled by the applicable app store or payment processor under their own privacy policies.

7. Data Storage and Security

Since game data remains on your device and we do not operate accounts, there is no personal profile of you held on our servers. For the limited technical data handled by our third-party providers, we implement reasonable measures and select providers that apply their own safeguards.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent any information. Limited technical data may be processed by:

  • Advertising networks.
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers.
  • App stores and payment processors.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

9. Data Retention

Game data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Technical data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own retention policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information from any user, no such data is gathered from children either.

11. Your Rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is generally no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You remain in full control of your game data:

  • Delete your game data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage from your device settings.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings.
  • Contact us at support@aidroid.co.in with any privacy question or request.

12. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as advertising networks, analytics and crash reporting providers, and app store payment processing. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

13. International Data Transfers

Technical data handled by our third-party providers may be processed and stored in countries outside your country of residence where data protection laws may differ.

14. Future Accounts and Features

We do not offer user accounts at this time. We may introduce optional accounts, cloud progress sync, or similar features in a future version of the App. If we do, we will update this Privacy Policy to describe what information is collected and how it is used, and we will obtain your consent where required by law before collecting any personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes become effective when posted within the App or on our website.
