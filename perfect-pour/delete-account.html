Delete Your Data

Last Updated: July 5, 2026

Perfect Pour: Brain Game does not use accounts. There is no sign-up or login, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. As a result, there is no account to delete and no personal profile of you stored on our servers.

Where Your Game Data Lives

All of your game information is saved locally on your own device, including:

  • Level progress, scores, stars and streaks.
  • In-game coins, hints, power-ups and achievements.
  • Game settings and saved preferences.

How to Delete Your Data

Because everything is stored on your device, you can remove it yourself at any time:

  • Android: Open Settings → Apps → Perfect Pour → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the App.
  • iOS: Touch and hold the Perfect Pour icon → Remove App → Delete App.

Uninstalling the App deletes your game data immediately and permanently.

What to Know Before Deleting

  • This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Your level progress and any unused coins, hints or power-ups will be lost, and we are unable to restore or transfer them.
  • If you purchased anything in the App, active subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting the App. Please cancel through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store to avoid future charges — see our Refund Policy.
  • If your device automatically backs up app data through Google or Apple, you may also need to remove that backup from your device or cloud settings.

Advertising Identifier

Our advertising and analytics partners may use your device's advertising identifier. You can reset or delete it at any time — on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If Accounts Are Added in Future

We may introduce optional accounts or cloud progress sync in a future version of the App. If we do, an in-app account deletion option will be provided and this page will be updated to explain how to use it.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about your data, or would like us to confirm what information is held, please reach out to our support team — we're happy to help.
